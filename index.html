Steady Closure, Late-Month Carry-Over

96.8% of September tickets closed with a solve time. All 10 still open were created between 23 and 30 Sep, and 7 of them in the final three days — normal month-end carry-over with no aged backlog.

Pending Tickets Awaiting Classification

The 6 tickets with no Category or Feature are all pending tickets that haven't been solved yet — 6 of the 10 open tickets. Only 1 solved ticket has no Organisation this month.

How-to Work Takes a Bigger Share of Time

How-to / Clarification tickets are 20.6% of volume but 32.2% of resolution hours (P90 217.9 hrs). Concern/Clarification carries the most hours of any category — 3,127 hrs across 44 tickets — and Call Logs questions have a 57.2-hr median.

Opportunity — Holiday Calendar Self-Serve

Holiday Calendar is again the biggest single driver: 73 tickets (23.5% of the month), median 0.9 hrs. Configuration / Admin is 55.9% of volume but 38.2% of resolution time — fast, repeatable work suited to self-serve.

Business Critical Long Tail

17 Business Critical / Phone Impacting tickets (5.5%), all closed. Median 3.2 hrs, but the P90 is 241.8 hrs — a few phone-impacting issues ran for over a week. Devices is the main driver (23 tickets, P90 214.6 hrs).

A Few Tickets Drive the Hours

Median resolution is 4.0 hrs, but 9 tickets ran past 300 hrs (12.5 days). They are 3% of solved tickets yet 32% of all resolution hours — led by #4717 Clarity Dental (693 hrs, Call Logs).

Recommended Operating Actions — October 2026
  • 1
    Close Out Classification Gaps

    The 6 tickets with no Category / Feature and 9 with no Ticket type are all pending tickets — make sure each is fully classified when it's solved. Only 1 solved ticket was missing an Organisation this month.

  • 2
    Call Logs & Clarification Playbook

    Concern/Clarification tickets took 3,127 hrs across 44 tickets (P90 303.5 hrs). Call Logs is the slowest common feature — 16 tickets, median 57.2 hrs, P90 359.7 hrs — including #4717 Clarity Dental at 693 hrs. A troubleshooting guide for call-log questions would cut the long tail.

  • 3
    Pilot Holiday Calendar Self-Serve

    Holiday Calendar is 73 tickets (23.5%) at a 0.9-hr median. With Agent App (46) and Agent setup (34), these three features are 153 tickets (49.2%) of September volume.

  • 4
    Rebalance Setup Work

    Advaith handled 24 of 26 New Config requests and 27 of 33 User setup & Access tickets. New Config requests have a 19.5-hr median, so spreading them across the team would reduce single-person dependency.

  • 5
    Business Critical & Devices Tail

    Business Critical is 17 tickets (5.5%), but its P90 is 241.8 hrs. Devices (23 tickets, P90 214.6 hrs) remains the main driver — keep the fast-track queue and review any device ticket open longer than 3 days.

  • 6
    Integration Tickets

    5 Integration tickets with a 172.3-hr median; 2 are still open with Advaith (#5052 First Choice Dental, #5089 Voicestack AU — both DNI Campaigns). #4800 Voicestack AU took 449 hrs. Agree an owner and escalation path for third-party waits.
